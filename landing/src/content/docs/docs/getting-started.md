Getting started with MockScores is easy to do. 
Accounts on MockScores can take on either of two roles: an **Organizer** or a **Coach**. 
Users can select what type of actions they would like to perform by selecting their role on the [home screen](https://app.mockscores.org).


## Signing in as an Organizer

Get started with MockScores by [creating an account](https://app.mockscores.org/register) or [logging in](https://app.mockscores.org/login) as an organizer. 
MockScores current supports logging in with a Username + Password or using an existing Google account.

## Signing in as a Coach

Coaches will be added to tournaments by Organizers. 
You will receive an email when your team is set up. Your account is linked to your email.
In order to access your team you must [sign up](https://app.mockscores.org/register) using the same email you were invited from.

## Signing in as a Scorer

Scorers do not create accounts. Before scoring a round, the organizer will assign the scorer to a trial.
Before the trial starts, the scorer will receive an email containing a unique link to their ballot.
Scorers will then enter their scores and award nominations.
Before coming to trial Scorers will need to make sure they bring a laptop or smartphone with them so they can access their email.
