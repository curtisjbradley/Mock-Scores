Organizers control when pairings and results become visible. A missing round usually means it has not yet been published to coaches.

## View the schedule

The **Schedule** page shows published rounds and their:

- Round name and time, or **Time TBD**
- Prosecution/plaintiff team
- Defense team
- Courtroom, or **TBD**
- Preparation status

Only published pairings appear. A lock badge means coaches can no longer edit roles or call orders.

## View the field

Select **Field** to see each participating team's code and name. Team codes are the identifiers used throughout schedules, scorer screens, results, and standings.

## View published results

Select **Results** after the organizer releases a round. If no released round exists, the page displays **No results published yet**.

When the tournament has a standings configuration, each side of a trial is shown with the same statistics the tournament uses, such as ballots won or point differential. Without that configuration, the page falls back to raw prosecution/plaintiff and defense points.

Select a trial row to expand its ballot details. From there, you can:

- View each ballot's prosecution/plaintiff and defense totals.
- Open an individual ballot.
- Open the combined scoresheet for the trial.

Individual award nominations appear to coaches only when the organizer enabled **Share individual rankings** in tournament settings.

## View standings

Select **Standings** to see:

- Current rank
- Team code and name
- The columns configured by the organizer
- A visual summary of the standings rules

The standings are calculated from results the tournament has released to coaches. They are informational and may change as later rounds are published or if the organizer corrects the configuration.

## Results troubleshooting

### A round is missing from Schedule

The organizer may not have published its pairings. Ask the organizer to confirm the round's publication status.

### A completed round is missing from Results

Results are published separately from pairings. The organizer may still be reviewing ballots or waiting for a missing submission.

### A ballot is unavailable

Confirm that the result has been published and try opening the trial from **Results** again. If a particular ballot is missing, contact the organizer with the round name and team codes.

### Standings are empty

Standings require submitted, published results and a valid tournament tiebreaker configuration. The organizer may still be preparing one of those items.
