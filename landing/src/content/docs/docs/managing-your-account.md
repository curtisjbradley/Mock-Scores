Organizers and coaches use MockScores accounts. Scorers who only open emailed ballot links do not need an account.

## Create and verify an account

Open [Create account](https://app.mockscores.org/register) and enter your first name, last name, email address, and password, or continue with Google.

A password must contain:

- At least eight characters
- At least one uppercase letter
- At least one number

When registering with email and password, follow the verification link sent to your email address. An invalid or expired verification link displays a failure message; return to sign in and contact MockScores support if you cannot verify the account.

Coaches must use the same email address the organizer invited. Registering under another address does not connect the account to the team.

## Reset a forgotten password

1. Open [Sign in](https://app.mockscores.org/login).
2. Select **Forgot Password?**
3. Enter your email address and select **Send reset link**.
4. Open the link in the email.
5. Enter and confirm a new password that meets the requirements.

For privacy, the reset page says to check your email even when no matching account is disclosed. If the message does not arrive, check spam, confirm the address, and try again.

A reset link may expire or become invalid after it is used. Request a new link instead of reusing an old one.

## Change your password

Open **Account** while signed in. Under **Change password**:

1. Enter the current password.
2. Enter a new password that meets the requirements.
3. Enter the new password again.
4. Select **Update password**.

The account page also displays the email address associated with the current session.

## Sign out

Open **Account** and select **Sign out**. On a shared computer, close the browser after signing out and do not save the password in the browser.

## Delete your account

Open the **Danger zone** on the account page and select **Delete account**. Review the warning, then select **Permanently Delete** only when you intend to remove the account and its associated data.

:::danger
Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. The confirmation warns that associated tournaments, teams, and scoring history will be removed. Transfer any responsibilities and export any records you need before deleting the account.
:::

Deleting an account is different from archiving a tournament. If you only want to hide a completed event from the active organizer list, archive the tournament instead.
